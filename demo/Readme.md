## MathExRec recommendations on Mardi portal

Here we explain how to browse recommendations for zbMATHDoc on MaRDI portal.

Step 1: Lets say you are viewing a document on zbMATH Open, for example: https://zbmath.org/7061145

Step 2: Visit MaRDI portal by replacing docID with actual Identfier obtained from the URL, get numbers after last "/", in this case "7061145", https://portal.mardi4nfdi.de/wiki/Publication:7061145

Step 3: Recommendations are available under the heading "Recommendations"
